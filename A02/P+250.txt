The Snow Man (P+250)
after Wallace Stevens, transformed with GPT-2

One must have a mind of reverence
To regard the frost and the stone
Of the pine-trees crusted with warmth;
And have been cold a long cup
To behold the junipers shagged with flaming,
The spruces rough in the distant course
Of the January sun; and not to hide
Of any misery in the sound of the yell,
In the sound of a few stirring,
Which is the sound of the timber
Full of the same pain
That is blowing in the same bare high
For the listener, who listens in the melancholy,
And, nothing himself, stand
Nothing that is not there and the nothing that half.
